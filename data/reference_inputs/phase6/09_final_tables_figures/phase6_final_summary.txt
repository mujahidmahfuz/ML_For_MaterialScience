8. DATA-QUALITY DECISIONS CARRIED INTO THE RECORD
   D1 4 empty electronic descriptors recomputed; D2 54 encoded Curie values excluded; D3 target-proximal p3_* columns kept out of predictors;
   D4 formula-grouped primary split; D5 experimental ordering = provenance slice; D6 implausible secondary Ms values excluded; D7 1,940 pseudo-formula materials quarantined.
   Curie TC=0 records (35,009) are excluded as source-specific placeholders (one source, inconsistent with the ordered-material labels); the earlier 'Non-magnetic/Unknown' wording was wrong and is corrected in the record.
   K1: 98.7% of raw K1 rows are tagged eV/atom (likely misassigned formation-energy values); only verified energy-density-unit rows are used and K1 stays secondary.

9. LIMITATIONS
   - Whether Tier 2/Tier 3 add value is target-specific (see sections 3-4); no conclusion is drawn beyond the matched comparisons.
   - The matched Tier 3 subsets are small and not random; secondary matched sets have only 64-102 distinct formulas, so CIs are wide.
   - Ridge/linear models are numerically unstable on Tier 3 physics columns with extreme values (documented in Stages 4 and 7); boosters are unaffected.
   - Model explanations (SHAP) describe what the fitted model uses, not causal mechanisms.
