Improvement = deeper tier minus Tier 1 performance, positive = better; identical matched rows, splits, grids and metrics at every tier; 10 prespecified formula-grouped splits. Intervals: Nadeau-Bengio corrected resampled-t 95% (refit-aware). Stable = this interval AND the pooled formula-bootstrap interval exclude 0 AND at least 7 of 10 splits share the sign; borderline = only one of the two intervals excludes 0; none = no stable change. Holm p over the primary family (9 Tier 1->3 contrasts: ordering, Curie, moment per cell x 3 algorithms). XGBoost = mean over 8 thread/seed realizations (Curie, moment per cell); single realization for ordering and the moment per formula unit. Curie Ridge uses train-fold 1st/99th-percentile clipping; unclipped Ridge is numerically unstable (-825 K) and is not interpretable. The Curie Ridge member of the Holm family is the unclipped contrast (adjusted p = 1.000); using the clipped contrast instead gives 0.740 for that contrast and leaves the other eight unchanged. The ordering gradient-boosting Tier 1->2 change is a borderline loss (the bootstrap interval excludes 0, the refit-aware interval does not). Moment per formula unit: 10,821 rows; 'beyond site count' = Tier 3 vs Tier 2 + the number of sites in the DFT cell.
