Analysis dataset v1.4 (frozen); formula-grouped 70/15/15 splits. Matched records = materials with complete Tier 3 descriptors, used at every tier. Ordering classes: FM 34,098, NM 31,673, FiM 7,161, AFM 6,193. The DFT moment is the Materials Project/JARVIS total moment of the computed cell at 0 K (not an experimental saturation magnetization); the moment per formula unit (m_fu = m_cell x atoms per formula unit / atoms in the cell, 10,821 matched rows) is analysed alongside. Tier 1 = 65 composition descriptors; Tier 2 = Tier 1 + 8 oxidation-state descriptors; Tier 3 = Tier 2 + 24 non-magnetic DFT descriptors (97).
