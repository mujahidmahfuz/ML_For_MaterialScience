## Notes

1. **No prior Tc or ordering study we read reports a chemistry-grouped test.** The splits are random, stratified-random or unspecified (Nelson & Sanvito; Long et al.; Abedi Orang et al.; NEMAD; Merker et al.). Composition-level de-duplication (median or mean Tc per composition; Nelson & Sanvito's L1 < 0.01 filter) is the furthest any of them goes. Belot et al. (DS1 -> DS2) and Acosta et al. (54-compound external 2D set) are the only external-dataset tests. Belot's DS2 overlaps DS1 by 1,189 compounds, so it is not a clean unseen-composition test.
2. **Reported Tc MAEs cluster at 50-70 K for random splits** (57 K Nelson & Sanvito; 58 K Long et al.; 69 K Belot et al.; 56-62 K NEMAD; about 42-49 for Abedi Orang Fig. 6, unit not printed). The one cross-dataset number (Belot et al., DS1 -> DS2) rises to 90 K. This is the baseline against which our grouped and external results (blind 465: MAE 102.7 K, R2 0.69; blind and unseen chemical system: R2 -0.17) can be placed.
3. **Ordering classification**: overall accuracies are 77.8% (Merker et al., balanced 3-class, DFT labels), 86% (Long et al., binary FM/AFM, experimental intermetallics) and 90-91% (NEMAD, 3-class). AFM is the weakest class wherever per-class numbers are given (NEMAD AFM F1 0.78-0.80; Merker AFM F1 0.69). None of these papers reports macro-F1 under grouped splits or calibration of class probabilities.
4. **Version caveat**: Nelson & Sanvito, Belot, Abedi Orang, Long, NEMAD and Acosta were read as arXiv preprints. Journal versions may differ in numbers (for example Long et al. arXiv v2 vs. the MRL version, and NEMAD arXiv v2 dated 24 Sep 2025 vs. Nat. Commun. 16, 9415 published 24 Oct 2025). Check against the journal versions before quoting in the final manuscript. Merker et al. and Court & Cole were read in their published open-access versions.
5. **Abedi Orang Fig. 6**: the MAE/RMSE values were read from rendered bar labels of Fig. 6 in arXiv:2509.17464v1. The axis has no unit; Kelvin is assumed but not stated. Their Dataset 2 is the external "malaei" table used as our external benchmark.
6. **Court & Cole** report no dataset-wide Tc regression metric. Their "MAE 3.1%" is a relative error on 5 orthochromite Neel temperatures.
7. **Belot et al.** report no R2 in the text that was read; only MAE and within-50/100 K fractions.
